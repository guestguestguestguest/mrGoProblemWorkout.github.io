How it works

Improve your strength through repetitive problem solving.

  • Import SGF problems

    A file, a whole folder, or a ZIP archive — one problem per file. Or start with the 24-problem Xuanxuan Qijing starter bundle.

  • Organize by collection & tag

    Every import becomes a collection. Add tags and favorites with a swipe.

  • Learn from your mistakes

    A built-in Most Difficult collection gathers the problems you miss most for extra attention.

  • Drill against the clock

    Workout your brain with timed runs, no hints. Best times are saved — and celebrated.

Guide

Everything is a tap or a swipe away.

  • Problems tab

    All problems in one list. Filter with the buttons at upper left. Swipe left to tag; swipe right to favorite. Tap + for import options.

  • Collections tab

    Swipe left to rename, merge or delete. Most Difficult and Favorites update automatically as you play.

  • Drills tab

    Tap +, pick problems, name it. Tap Run to solve against the clock. Long-press a problem to preview it.

  • Settings

    Drill size (min/max), board color, sound, hints, random rotation and more.

  • Writing your own SGF

    Starting position plus correct moves. With multiple paths, comment each final position right or wrong.

  • “No verdict” problems

    Run Maintenance › Find Problems with Unresolved Paths, fix the file, then swipe right › Replace SGF.
